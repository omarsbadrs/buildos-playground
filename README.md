# BuildOs Playground

**Try it: https://buildos-playground.vercel.app**

A public sandbox of the BuildOs 3D modeler — draw walls, slabs, columns, beams, roofs and stairs, place
engineering and architecture families, shape the site, and open the sample projects (a substation, a
gas-turbine plant, towers), in 3D and 2D, right in the browser.

- **No sign-up.** Your browser gets its own private sandbox: nobody else sees your projects.
- **Temporary.** Projects are deleted after 24 hours (5 at a time per browser).
- **Modeling only.** The full BuildOs platform (4D/5D planning and cost, dashboards, the AI assistant
  and accounts) is not part of the playground.

This repository holds only the playground's **built website** (static files deployed by Vercel). The
BuildOs source code is private; this playground runs against its own sandbox engine, separate from the
real platform and its data.
